# Lesson 1 Quiz — What Is Social Service Work?

Try these without looking at the lesson first.

1. In your own words, what is the main role of a Social Service Worker?
2. What is the difference between helping and rescuing?
3. What does client self-determination mean?
4. Give two examples of healthy professional boundaries.
5. Name three ethical principles important in social service work.
6. What is a strengths-based approach?
7. Put these stages in order: intervention, engagement, evaluation, assessment, termination/transition, planning.
8. A client says, “Tell me what I should do.” What could you say that supports self-determination instead of making the decision for them?
9. Why is confidentiality important?
10. What is the one thing a worker should remember when helping someone in crisis?

## Answer guide
1. To support people in understanding problems, accessing resources, building strengths, and working toward goals while respecting their choices.
2. Helping supports the client’s participation and independence; rescuing takes over and can create dependence.
3. Clients have the right to make their own choices when legally and safely able to do so.
4. Examples: not lending money, limiting personal disclosure, keeping communication professional, following agency rules, avoiding romantic/sexual relationships with clients.
5. Examples: respect, dignity, confidentiality, honesty, fairness, competence, professional responsibility, avoiding harm.
6. It focuses on abilities, resources, resilience, and past successes as well as problems.
7. Engagement → Assessment → Planning → Intervention → Evaluation → Termination/transition.
8. Example: “I can help you look at the options and consequences. Which option feels most workable to you?”
9. It protects client privacy, trust, dignity, and the professional relationship, subject to legal and safety limits.
10. Work with the client rather than taking control of the client’s life.
