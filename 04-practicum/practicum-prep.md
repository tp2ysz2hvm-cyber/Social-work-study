# Practicum Preparation

## Before placement
- Review agency policies and confidentiality expectations.
- Know your student role and limits.
- Learn who your supervisor is and how supervision works.
- Bring questions rather than guessing.
- Track required placement hours carefully.

## Weekly reflection
- What did I observe?
- What did I do?
- What went well?
- What was difficult?
- What feelings came up for me?
- What social work skill or theory connects to this experience?
- What will I do differently next time?
- What should I discuss in supervision?

## Professional habits
- Be reliable and on time.
- Protect client privacy.
- Document objectively.
- Ask for feedback.
- Use supervision when uncertain.
- Maintain clear boundaries.
