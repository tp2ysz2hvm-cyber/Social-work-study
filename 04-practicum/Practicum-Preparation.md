# Practicum Preparation

## Before placement
- Learn the agency's mandate and population served.
- Review confidentiality and documentation expectations.
- Understand your student role and limits.
- Identify learning goals with your supervisor.
- Prepare questions for supervision.

## During placement
- Arrive prepared and on time.
- Ask when unsure rather than guessing.
- Protect client confidentiality.
- Keep professional boundaries.
- Take useful notes according to agency policy.
- Request feedback and apply it.

## Weekly reflection
1. What did I observe or do this week?
2. What went well?
3. What challenged me?
4. What social service skill or concept did I use?
5. What would I do differently next time?
6. What do I want to ask my supervisor?
