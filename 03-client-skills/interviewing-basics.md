# Interviewing Basics

## Core skills
- Open-ended questions
- Active listening
- Reflection of feeling
- Paraphrasing
- Summarizing
- Clarifying
- Appropriate silence
- Non-judgmental language
- Respect for client choice and autonomy

## A simple interview flow
1. Introduce yourself and your role.
2. Explain the purpose of the conversation.
3. Ask what brought the person in or what they would like help with.
4. Listen for facts, feelings, strengths, risks, supports, and goals.
5. Clarify anything important without interrogating.
6. Summarize what you heard.
7. Agree on next steps.

## Questions that usually work well
- What has been happening lately?
- How has that affected you?
- What feels most important right now?
- What have you already tried?
- Who or what has been helpful?
- What would you like to be different?

## Avoid
- Rapid-fire questions
- Making promises you cannot keep
- Giving advice before understanding the situation
- Judging, blaming, or arguing
- Assuming you know what the client means
