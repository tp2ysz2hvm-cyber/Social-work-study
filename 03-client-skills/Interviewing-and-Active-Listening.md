# Interviewing and Active Listening

## Core skills
- Open-ended questions
- Reflection
- Paraphrasing
- Clarification
- Summarizing
- Empathy
- Appropriate silence
- Non-judgmental language
- Professional boundaries

## Simple interview flow
1. Introduce yourself and your role.
2. Explain the purpose of the conversation.
3. Ask what brought the client in or what they would like help with.
4. Listen before trying to solve the problem.
5. Clarify important facts.
6. Identify needs, strengths, supports, and concerns.
7. Agree on realistic next steps.
8. Summarize and close.

## Practice reminder
Do not turn an interview into an interrogation. Ask one useful question, listen carefully to the answer, and let the next question grow naturally from what the client says.
